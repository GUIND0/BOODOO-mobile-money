### Cancellation and recovery
Transfer caller POST `/v1/transfers/{id}/cancellation` with `transfers:cancel` and the original caller subject. CANCELLING remains pending until the ledger reference barrier proves CANCELLED and the limits reference barrier prevents late reservations and releases existing usage. A POSTED ledger outcome is completed normally; it is never refunded by cancellation. Requires the journal cancellation barrier PR and `ledger:journals:cancel` on dedicated credentials. Limits reference cancellation requires `limits:reservations:settle` and the original reserving subject. No elapsed timeout or 404 alone releases uncertain money. Enable scheduled recovery with `BOODOO_RECOVERY_ENABLED=true`; bounded batches of 25 persisted authorized intents use database leases, skip CREATED without PIN and retry every 15 seconds. Deployment workers require provisioned tenant service credentials. Cancellation barriers are permanent.
