### Merchant payments
A signed operator with `merchants:write` registers `{id,walletId,code}` at POST `/v1/merchants`. The recipient must be a currently eligible wallet; identity and wallet binding are immutable and unique per tenant. Service read uses `merchants:read`. Operator `merchants:control` POSTs `/internal/v1/merchants/{id}/state` with `{id:eventUUID,state:ACTIVE|SUSPENDED,expectedVersion,reasonCode}`.

POST `/v1/merchant-payments` with `payments:write`, Idempotency-Key and `{merchantId,senderWalletId,amount,maxFee,pin}`. It resolves the registered wallet then uses the same durable PIN/fee/risk/limits/ledger P2P workflow. Location points to the resulting transfer for status and recovery. Merchant keys have a separate namespace from direct transfers. Suspension prevents new merchant-endpoint requests, including endpoint replays; use GET transfer for historical results. Suspension does not revoke already accepted financial intent or block direct incoming transfers; use accounting freeze for an immediate hold. Merchant commercial onboarding remains an operator responsibility.
