### Full linked reversals
Operator API: POST `/internal/v1/journals/{id}/reversal` with `{"reasonCode":"DUPLICATE_SETTLEMENT"}` and `ledger:reversals:request`; then a **different signed operator subject** POSTs `/internal/v1/reversals/{id}/approval` with `ledger:reversals:approve`. Read with `ledger:reversals:read`. The full original entries are flipped once and approval commits atomically with that compensation. Insufficient available funds leave approval pending with 409; there is no overdraft or history rewrite. Original journal identity provides natural idempotency; reasons/maker cannot change. Partial refunds and reversal of compensation journals are unsupported.

Physical cash settlement journals produced by distribution-service are excluded from generic reversals. Their return/failed-disbursement workflow needs provider/cash evidence coordination and must not race a cashier releasing physical funds. The pilot does not automatically reverse external cash settlement.
