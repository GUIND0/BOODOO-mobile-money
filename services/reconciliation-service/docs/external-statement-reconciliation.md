### External settlement statements
POST `/internal/v1/external-statements` with service scope `reconciliation:statements:write`, ledger audience and `ledger:statements:read`. Body: `{id,accountId,provider,statementReference,from,to,opening,closing,entries:[{externalId,reference,side,amount}]}`. All values are XOF integer minor units; DEBIT increases the settlement ASSET, CREDIT decreases it (normalize the bank's own sign conventions before import). Entries reference BOODOO journal reference UUIDs. Provider/statement identity and source evidence are immutable. This API imports an integrator-supplied bank statement; bank download and authenticity verification remain the integrator's responsibility.

Periods are half-open [from,to), at most 31 days, ending at least 60 seconds in the past. The ledger supplies one repeatable-read snapshot, bounded at 500 entries. Truncated or unsettled windows produce INCOMPLETE, never MATCHED. Matching compares every reference/side/amount and both opening/closing balances. Input arithmetic and duplicate references/external IDs are checked. GET report needs `reconciliation:statements:read`.

A signed operator with `reconciliation:investigate`, a provisioned `RECONCILIATION_SERVICE_TOKENS_JSON` tenant token map (signed sub=reconciliation-service, actor_type=service, ledger journal-read audience/scope) can POST `/{id}/investigations` with `{id:eventUUID,reasonCode,relatedJournalId}`. The journal must exist for this tenant. An investigation note does not clear or rewrite the discrepancy. Correct accounting through independently approved linked reversals, then import a new statement/report identity for new evidence; automatic money adjustments are prohibited. Closed-period completeness still depends on the integrator's settlement cut-off and late-posting controls.
